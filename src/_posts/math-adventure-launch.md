Welcome to the official launch of **Math Adventure**!

When we started designing Math Adventure at Bihobol, we asked ourselves a simple question: *What would the ideal educational experience look like if it were built solely for the child, without any compromises for advertising algorithms or commercial monetization?*

The answer became our guiding compass:
1. **Zero Distractions:** No banner commercials, no video popups, and no paywalls.
2. **Offline Accessibility:** Works anywhere without an active internet connection.
3. **Pure Educational Fun:** An intuitive progression from early counting to arithmetic and geometry.

---

## 1. Designed for Ages 2 to 10

Early math education shouldn't feel like a series of repetitive drills. Math Adventure turns math into an exciting quest across several foundational subjects:

* **Counting & Shapes:** Visual identification of numbers and geometry for toddlers and preschoolers.
* **Addition & Subtraction:** Tactile number bonds with cheerful animations and instant positive reinforcement.
* **Multiplication & Division:** Mental math challenges designed to build confidence in elementary learners.

---

## 2. Privacy is Our Non-Negotiable Foundation

As parents and creators, we believe children's digital safety is paramount. Math Adventure operates with **zero personal data collection**:

* No names, email addresses, or phone numbers are requested.
* No hardware identifiers, GPS locations, or ad tracking IDs (IDFA / AD_ID) are read.
* All game progress and settings reside strictly on your local device storage.

We are proud to strictly comply with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, **GDPR-K**, and the **Google Play Families Policy**.
