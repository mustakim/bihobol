## 1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to **Math Adventure**! We believe that children's educational games must provide a joyful, enriching, and above all, completely safe and private environment. 

This Privacy Policy explains how **Math Adventure** ("we", "our", or "the app") treats user information. Our guiding principle is simple: **we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information from children or adults.**

Math Adventure is designed for young children (toddlers through elementary students, ages 2 to 10) and their families. We fully comply with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**, the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K)**, the **Google Play Families Policy**, and the **Apple App Store Kids Category Guidelines**.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Math Adventure operates entirely offline without requiring user accounts or external network connectivity. Specifically:

* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or dates of birth.
* **No Device Identifiers:** We do not collect, read, or track hardware IDs (IMEI, MAC address, Android ID) or Advertising Identifiers (Google Advertising ID / AD_ID, Apple IDFA).
* **No Geolocation Data:** We do not access, track, or record your physical location or GPS coordinates.
* **No Biometrics, Camera, or Audio Recordings:** The app does not access your camera, microphone, photos, media library, or contacts.
* **No Analytics or Telemetry:** We do not bundle third-party tracking SDKs, analytics suites (such as Firebase Analytics, Flurry, Adjust, or AppsFlyer), or crash telemetry loggers that transmit data off your device.
* **No Internet Transmission:** The game functions completely offline. No data generated within the app is ever transmitted to any remote server or cloud database.

---

## 3. Local Data Stored on Your Device

To provide a seamless, personalized experience, Math Adventure saves a small amount of non-sensitive configuration data **locally on your device** using local storage (`SharedPreferences`). This data **never leaves your device**:

1. **Player / Hero Name (Optional):**
   * If entered in the Settings screen, a nickname or first name (e.g., "Alex", "Sophia") is stored locally on the device to display a friendly greeting (e.g., *"Welcome, Sophia! ⭐"*).
   * Providing a name is entirely optional. Default gameplay is completely anonymous.
2. **Game Progress & Unlocked Stages:**
   * The highest stage completed across subjects (*Counting, Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, Colors & Shapes*) is stored locally so progress is not lost when restarting the application.
3. **Audio Settings:**
   * Your chosen background music volume level ($0\% - 100\%$) and mute toggle state are saved locally to maintain your preferred listening volume between sessions.

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Protecting young children online is our highest priority:

* **Zero Personal Information:** We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area / UK).
* **No Social Features or Chat:** There are no chat rooms, forums, direct messaging, or multi-player interactions.
* **No External Links Without Parental Consent:** The app contains no links to external websites, social networks, or unvetted web pages.
* **No Behavioral Profiling:** We do not profile, track, or monitor children across apps, devices, or browsing sessions.

---

## 5. Advertising & In-App Purchases

* **No Advertisements:** Math Adventure is **100% ad-free**. We do not display third-party advertisements, banner ads, interstitial popups, or rewarded video commercials.
* **No In-App Purchases:** The entire educational curriculum is fully accessible without microtransactions, paywalls, or digital subscriptions.

---

## 6. Permissions Requested

Math Adventure respects device integrity and does not request any dangerous or runtime Android / iOS permissions. The application requires only:

* **Audio Output:** Standard audio playback permissions for sound effects and background melodies.
* **Local Storage / Sandbox:** Standard application sandbox storage for retaining game progress and local settings.

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

All game progress and settings reside exclusively inside the application's isolated local sandbox on your device.

* **How to Delete Data:** You may reset or permanently delete all data at any time by:
  1. Opening your device **Settings** > **Apps** > **Math Adventure** > **Storage** > **Clear Data / Clear Storage**, or
  2. Uninstalling the app from your device.
* Uninstalling Math Adventure instantly and permanently purges all saved stages, hero names, and preferences with no residual data remaining on any server.

## 8. Third-Party Services

Because Math Adventure does not integrate external analytics, advertisement networks, or remote cloud databases, **no third parties have access to your data or activity through this application.**
